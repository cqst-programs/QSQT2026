About the Conference

QSQT2026 is a premier international gathering dedicated to the rapidly evolving landscape of quantum systems, quantum computing, and quantum information technologies. The conference brings together leading researchers, industry pioneers, and young scientists to share breakthroughs and shape the future of quantum science.

This year's edition will feature keynote speeches from Nobel laureates, hands-on workshops, panel discussions on quantum-safe cryptography, and dedicated poster sessions. Whether you are a theoretical physicist, quantum engineer, or tech entrepreneur — QSQT2026 offers an unparalleled platform for collaboration and discovery.

Quantum Hardware

Superconducting qubits, trapped ions, photonics & topological systems.

Algorithms & Software

Quantum advantage, error mitigation, hybrid classical‑quantum models.

Global Impact

Policy, quantum education, and emerging quantum ecosystems.
