## About the Conference

QSQT 2026 focuses on recent advances in:

- Quantum Computing
- Quantum Information
- Quantum Communication
- Quantum Machine Learning
- Quantum Materials
- Quantum Cryptography

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, students, and industry experts working in quantum technologies.
